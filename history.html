THE
EVOLUTION

From blinking screens to massive virtual worlds: the story of how gaming evolved.

1970s

THE BEGINNING

Early arcade and home experiments proved that computers could be used for interactive entertainment. Simple graphics and electronic sounds were enough to create a new medium.

1980s

THE ARCADE BOOM

Arcades became cultural landmarks. High-score chasing, colourful cabinets and instantly recognisable characters helped establish gaming as mainstream entertainment.

1990s

THE 16-BIT → 3D SHIFT

Home consoles became more powerful, CD technology expanded game size, and 3D graphics transformed what developers could build. Multiplayer and fighting games became huge social experiences.

2000s

ONLINE GAMING

Broadband internet changed how games were played. Consoles gained online services, while PC gaming developed enormous communities around competitive and cooperative games.

2010s

MOBILE + STREAMING

Smartphones put games into millions of pockets. Digital stores became the norm and game streaming, esports and video platforms helped gaming become a global spectator culture.

2020s

IMMERSION & CREATION

Powerful consoles, VR, procedural systems, cross-platform play and creator tools continue to blur the line between playing, watching and making games.

THEN VS NOW
