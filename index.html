WHAT WAS GAMING LIKE IN THE 90s?

1990 → 1999
🕹️

ARCADES

Arcades were social hotspots. You fed coins into machines, battled friends for high scores and learned games one life at a time.

📺

HOME CONSOLES

16-bit consoles gave bedrooms their own gaming worlds. Cartridges, wired controllers and split-screen multiplayer were everywhere.

💿

A NEW ERA

3D graphics, CDs and bigger worlds arrived. Gaming started moving from simple scores towards stories, characters and exploration.
